Journal and watchlist are saved only in this browser (not shared, not uploaded). Clearing site data erases them. P&L is shown per currency and ignores brokerage, taxes and slippage.
LONG: entry = last session high + 1 tick, stop below. SHORT: entry = last session low − 1 tick, stop above. Stop = 2×ATR(14) of 5-min bars away from entry, T1 = 1R, T2 = 2R. Shares = the smallest of four limits: max loss, position cap (75% of account), max order (80%), cash after a 10% buffer. Indian stocks are NSE (Nifty 100 + Bank Nifty universe) sized on the ₹ account; foreign stocks are US, sized on the $ account. Long setups: 20-day breakout, bull flag, reclaim of 20-day average, oversold bounce, opening-range breakout. Short setups are the mirror: 20-day breakdown, bear flag, loss of 20-day average, overbought rejection, opening-range breakdown. Shorting needs a broker that allows it (intraday in India; margin account in the US) and carries unlimited-loss risk. Market status ignores exchange holidays. A matched rule is not a trade signal. Stops are plans, not guarantees. Flat by 15:55 ET (US) / 15:15 IST (India). Click a column header to sort.
